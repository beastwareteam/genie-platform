# Accessibility

!!! info "Machine translation"

    This page was translated automatically from the German original. Wording may be imprecise; the German version is authoritative.

This explanation describes how accessible these pages and the application are — and where they
are not. The second part is the more important one: a declaration that full compliance
is worthless on the first rebuttal.

**Scale** is EN 301 549 as amended; it refers to the
Guidelines for accessible web content (WCAG) in level AA. Legal basis in German
The law is the Accessibility Strengthening Act.

## Two surfaces, two stands

This statement concerns two things that do not have the same status. To mix them would be
misleading.

### These pages (documentation)

| Requirement | Status |
| --- | --- |
| Headings without jumps, exactly one main heading per page | complied with, machine-tested |
| Text alternatives to images | complied with — there are currently no images |
| Language label per page | prepared, not yet delivered |
| Contrast, keyboard operation, visible focus | depends on the presentation, which is not yet delivered |
| Subtitles and text alternative to videos | no videos available yet; none will be published without subtitles |

The heading check runs with every change. It is not an intention, but a
Condition: a page that violates this does not pass.

### The application itself

**Unaudited.** For software, a separate part of the standard applies — names and roles for each
Operating element, a sensible sequence for keyboard operation, sufficient contrasts. The
Application brings with it over 140 building blocks; a test on all of them has **not** taken place.

What is available and will help:

- Colours are not assigned individually, but centrally. A contrast test must therefore be carried out on
in one place, not in hundreds.
- A high-contrast colour scheme has already been created and can be selected.

What is missing: the measurement against the threshold values of the standard, and the review of the modules on
Names, roles, and keyboard order.

## Whether the law applies here

The Accessibility Enhancement Act covers products and services that consumers
offered. The platform is not currently offered in this form. Should the
change, the law applies — and the unchecked status described above would then not be
Intermediate state more, but a deficiency.

We therefore already describe the status now, instead of waiting for someone to claim it.

## Feedback

and what you wanted to achieve. We do not treat feedback on barriers as
Wish, but as a finding.
